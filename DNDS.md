# D&DS integration

Dogfights and Deep Space uses the author's 85-page source unchanged at `public/systems/dnds/dnds-original.pdf`. Readable text is imported with `scripts/import-dnds.py` (PyMuPDF); unmapped quote glyphs are normalized only in readable text. The source SHA-256 is recorded and checked by `npm.cmd run test:references`. Lore, personal equipment, classes and races have heading navigation. Pages 25-55 are excluded from reader results and remain in the PDF: no ship combat routes, catalog entries or engine were added.

## Character foundation

2014 5E/SRD 5.1 defines max level 20, ability scores, saving throws, HP, point buy, progression and rests unless the source overrides it. Technology (Intelligence) replaces Arcana. Setting race names preserve their 5E counterpart mechanics: Eldar/High Elf, Half-Eldar/Half-Elf, Votaan/Hill Dwarf, Nocturni/Tiefling, Orak-Hai/Half-Orc, Dargonian/Dragonborn, Saturnian/Rock Gnome, Uranian/Lightfoot Halfling. Drukhari use Drow +2 Dexterity/+1 Charisma; conditional racial traits are reference-based. Omnics use a Human foundation because no distinct statblock is supplied.

Battle Medic maps to Cleric and Heavy Gunner to Paladin. Artificer has a d8/Intelligence-Constitution foundation and its defined Technological Services feature; detailed infusions and subclasses are manual, rather than copying a non-SRD class text. Other classes retain SRD progression and receive the document's custom feature descriptions. Monk Technology proficiency and Cleric/Paladin extra armor/weapon training initialize automatically. Additional proficiencies are editable in the character editor.

Humanoid Druid practice, psionic amplification, Monk hacking, Wizard alien expertise, sniper action refunds and other contextual effects remain explicit feature text for GM adjudication. Monk-Warlock Synergy has one use per long rest. The source's contradictory lore names remain unchanged. Spell-slot selection, advanced class choices and conditional racial effects remain reference-based, as in the plain 5E system.

## Equipment and attacks

41 personal weapons, four armor options and nine ammunition variants are imported. Damage, Reload, Misfire, Recoil, Rate of Fire, Penetration, Melting, Irradiating and area keywords follow the source. Missing weights, prices and ranges are reasonable **companion estimates**, clearly distinguished from source values. Armor has an estimated 5E base AC/Dexterity cap. Editor fields allow changing weight, price, range, capacity, remaining charges, suppression and armor AC/cap. Equipped armor displays AC/resistance; multiple equipped armors prompt selection rather than stacking AC. Class-specific AC exceptions remain manual.

Use the summary's Weapon attack settings to enter target AC, distance, proficiency, roll mode, dual wield and mounting. Equip a weapon and Attack to save a firing action and show its roll/damage. Automatic hits are `(1 + floor(max(0, attackTotal - AC) / recoil)) * rateOfFire`; Dexterity damage is added once per attack. Natural 1 misses, natural 20 hits and doubles damage dice. Range/scoped advantage and disadvantage cancel normally. Ranger pistol/SMG/sniper training doubles attack proficiency. Suppressors halve range and reduce damage by 25%, except for Rogues. Shotgun close range adds 50% damage. Heavy weapons require training and Strength modifier +2 unless mounted or Power Armor is equipped.

A chosen natural result at or below Misfire reports a **JAM** and spends a firing action. No persistent jam state or repair workflow is imposed. Tinkering is an Intelligence or Dexterity check at the player's choice; Artificers clear ordinary jams without a check. Attack results and jam messages are shown as dismissible notifications only after a successful save. Failed saves retain exact rolls and charge changes for retry.

Standard ammunition is unlimited/free. Reload restores firing charges and consumes one quantity of selected special ammo; quantity represents a complete reload. Inferno weapons reject these nonstandard projectile-ammo variants. Basic elemental ammo adds its extra damage die per hit; compound weapons use their first listed damage die as the fallback. Hollow Point/Armor Piercing target classification, explosive area victims, Psychoactive psychosis, resistances, penetration targets, armor melting and radiation stacks require GM resolution. Area weapons report damage and their specified save DC; per-target outcomes stay manual. Reload action cost and Sniper Chain are displayed/rule text rather than a turn scheduler.

Character saves preserve existing unknown data and use optimistic concurrency. The only database change adds the system catalog row; ownership, RLS and same-system campaign constraints continue to apply. The adapter and copied inventory definitions separate future revisions from existing character snapshots.
